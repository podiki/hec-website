### Come celebrate Hawthorne Garden

#### When: Sunday, November 17th, 2024
#### Time: Noon to 3 pm
#### Where: Hawthorne Garden

Greetings from HEC’s Community Organizer, Stan Horwitz.

Last month, the Board of the Philadelphia Redevelopment Authority (PRA) approved a resolution transferring Hawthorne Garden to the Neighborhood Gardens Trust (NGT) with a deed restriction stating that the garden must be used as a community garden. This is wonderful news! It means the garden is protected and won’t be taken away as long as we operate it properly as a garden. 

We appreciate all the support you have offered us. We want to celebrate!!! Thanks also to City Council [President Kenyatta Johnson](https://phlcouncil.com/kenyattajohnson/) for making this wonderful turn of events happen.

**Please come to Hawthorne Garden to celebrate**

Sunday, November 17th  Noon to 3pm

We’ll have food, drink, and good conversation. We hope you can stop by to say hi!
