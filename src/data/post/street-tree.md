### Show your street some love!

#### Deadline: November 15th, 2024
#### Apply here: https://pg-cloud.com/phs/?openform=request-a-tree

Greetings from HEC’s Community Organizer, Stan Horwitz.

This is a reminder that you can apply to have the Philadelphia Horticultural Society’s Tree Tenders plant a free tree in front of your house.

The deadline to apply for your free tree is November 15th.

To apply for your free street tree, click on the following link:

https://pg-cloud.com/phs/?openform=request-a-tree

If you have any questions about this program, please contact your local Tree Tenders group leader or the PHS Trees Team (trees@pennhort.org).

#### Please share this announcement with your neighbors.

If you have any questions about Hawthorne or you would like to volunteer with the Hawthorne Empowerment Coalition, please send an email to info@hecphila.org or call us at (267) 209-3423.
