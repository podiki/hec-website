HEC is currently in the process of revising and updating our bylaws! This page contains information about the process and progress. To get in touch with the Board about the bylaws and this process, please email <a href="mailto:info@hecphila.org">info@hecphila.org</a>.

A recent presentation about key proposed changes can be found at [this link](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1OqN175iVP-aOjgl0TeOZi-YXBdERnnybiw9dNY-Baq8/edit?usp=sharing) (or as [a PDF](../files/ModernizingHECBylaws.pdf)).

## Why Now & Our Objectives 
- Bylaws outdated – no updates since 2002 
- Restrictive voting provisions have made it difficult to reflect community input and elect permanent Board members 
- Numerous confusing and contradictory provisions 
- Opportunity to modernize bylaws to create a strong and sustainable HEC 
- Ensure bylaws maximize community input, engagement, and transparency 
- Incorporate best practices from high performing Philadelphia Registered  Community Organization (RCOs) 
- Build in flexibility to accommodate growth in HEC goals and activities 
- Pass new bylaws well in advance of next year’s Board election

## Where Community Input Happens 
Community input is an important part of the Hawthorne Empowerment Coalition’s bylaws review process. This guide explains when and how community members can participate, ask questions, and share feedback. This guide is intended to support transparency and participation throughout the bylaws review process.

1. **Community Meetings**

The draft bylaws are presented at both in-person and Zoom community meetings. These meetings are opportunities to hear an overview of the proposed changes, ask questions, and understand how the process works.

2. **Question & Discussion Periods**

Time is set aside during meetings for questions and clarification. This helps ensure the proposed bylaws are understood before feedback is submitted.

3. **Written Feedback Window**

Following the community meetings, a written feedback period is opened. Community members can submit comments, questions, and suggestions using an online form.

4. **Review of Feedback**

All feedback submitted during the open window is reviewed by the Bylaws Committee and shared with the Board as part of the revision process.

5. **What Community Input Can Influence**

Community feedback helps shape clarity, structure, and implementation details of the bylaws. The Board considers this input carefully when finalizing the draft.

6. **What Is Legally Board-Controlled**

Some decisions are governed by legal and structural requirements. While community input is valued, final decisions must comply with the organization’s legal obligations and existing bylaws.
