In accordance with the 2002 Bylaws, the Hawthorne Empowerment Coalition (HEC) is seeking up to 15 dedicated neighbors to serve on the 2026-2027 Board of Directors. Since 1999, HEC has served as the civic voice for our community, advocating for smart development and the preservation of Hawthorne Park. Our nomination process relies on you to identify leaders committed to enhancing the quality of life from Broad to 11th Street and from South Street to Washington Ave. Once nominations are collected, the community will review candidates and vote. Please use this form to submit your nominations and help shape the future of Hawthorne. Elections will be in June 2026.
