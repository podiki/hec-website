Funded by a community benefits agreement with Post Brothers, the Hawthorne Empowerment Coalition’s Continuing Education Scholarship supports students from the Academy at Palumbo, the High School For Creative & Performing Arts, or a high school student who resides in the Hawthorne neighborhood as they transition into college or technical training. HEC will award two scholarships for $1500 each. These funds are intended to help students cover common costs that arise with starting post high school education such as purchasing a new computer, books, or other academic supplies, moving expenses, or furnishing cost for students leaving home for the first time.

## How To Apply
To be eligible, students must be a graduating high school senior who either lives in or attends high school in the Hawthorne neighborhood and who plans to continue their education after graduations. This could be at a community college, a four year college or university, or a technical training program after graduation. To apply for the scholarship, you need to submit the following by **May 8th**.

- A completed [application form](https://forms.gle/iYEayvm1fxf6f5TY7)
- One letter of recommendation from a teacher, a coach, a religious leader, or another adult who knows you well. Parents or other family members are not eligible to submit letters of recommendation.
- A letter from your school’s counselor attesting that you are on track for graduation and will attend a college, university, or technical training program after graduation.
- A 500 words or less statement describing your education goals after graduation and how you will use the HEC College Transition Scholarship help you transition to college.

**Application Form**: https://forms.gle/ixxsUEBkEXfcGnN4A

**Letter Submission Form**: https://forms.gle/Ter7ubsoNDG5jb2h8

Students will be selected based on their letters of recommendation and their personal statement, with special consideration placed on the impact the scholarship will have on easing the transition to further education. Priority will be given to students who live in the Hawthorne neighborhood, but all Palumbo and CAPA students are also eligible to receive the funds and are encouraged to apply.

**Due Date: May 8th**

#### What should I write in my statement?
First tell us what your plans and ambitions are for your education. Tell us where you are going to go, what you are planning to study, and what you are hoping to prepare for. Tell us why continuing your education is important to you and what you are hoping to gain from the experience for yourself and your community. Finally, tell us how the $1500 will support you in transitioning to the next step in your education.  For example, you could say if you are planning to buy a new computer, purchase special equipment for your classes, or help with the expenses of moving to campus.

#### Who should write my letter?
Your letter of recommendation should come from an adult, outside your family, that knows you well. Common options are a high school teacher, a sports or music coach, an acting director, the religious leader at your place of worship, or the head of an organization you volunteer with. It is important that you pick a letter writer who knows you well and can paint a vivid picture of who you are in your community. You should share this application with your letter writer, especially the guidelines below. Your letter writer will submit the letters directly to the HEC Education committee ([link here](https://forms.gle/swx7JNPXiMDzPi3r8)). They should **not** give the letter to you to submit.

#### Guidelines for letter writers
- Letters should be one to two pages
- Tell us who you are, what your relationship to the applicant is, and how long you have known them.
- We want to get a sense of who the applicants are in their communities. Share with us what you think makes them special and remarkable. Tell us how they liven and improve the spaces they inhabit.
- Tell us about the traits, attributes, or skills that will help the applicant thrive in their continued education.
- If you can, discuss the impact the scholarship will have on transitioning the student to the next step in their education.
- School Counselors only need to submit a letter attesting that the applicant is on track to graduate and will attend a college, university, or technical training program after graduation.

**Submit your letters here**: https://forms.gle/K6PXxfN1MmUesLHc8
